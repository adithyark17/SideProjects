The Problem

02 — FRAGMENTATION

India's technology employment market is fragmented

Disconnected platforms today
01
LinkedIn and job boards — for open roles
02
Salary platforms — for compensation
03
Review platforms — for culture
04
Startup databases — for funding
05
News sites — for expansion announcements
06
Social media — for interview & workplace info
The result
—
Data is fragmented across sources
—
Salary information lacks context
—
Job listings do not explain company quality
—
Employee reviews are often noisy
—
GCC mandates are difficult to understand
—
Candidates struggle to compare offers objectively

Core problem: there is no trusted intelligence layer for deciding where to work in India's technology economy.

Why Now

03 — MARKET SHIFTS

India is entering a new employment-intelligence era

Three shifts creating this opportunity
01
Global capability centres are expanding
Global companies are rapidly expanding capability centres across India.
02
Startups and GCCs compete for the same talent
Both are chasing the same pool of high-quality technology professionals.
03
Candidates want evidence, not vibes
Professionals increasingly want evidence before changing jobs.
Key market signals
—
Rapid GCC growth in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, NCR, Mumbai
—
More product, AI, analytics, finance, cybersecurity mandates moving to India
—
Greater candidate interest in compensation transparency
—
Higher demand for company- and city-level employment data
—
Increasing complexity comparing RSUs, ESOPs, bonuses, ownership, stability
