Long-term view — 5 to 10 years

With disciplined execution, this can become a ₹500–1,000 crore branded food company.

Achieving that scale requires sustained investment in product quality, brand building, distribution, and manufacturing excellence — the ambition is conditional on execution, not guaranteed by market size alone.

For maximum probability of success: compete on authenticity, superior taste, premium quality, convenience, and regional culinary expertise — never on price.
